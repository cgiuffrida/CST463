PA3 CNN EXPERIMENT SUMMARY
Status: October 25, 2026

SETUP
We built a configurable notebook for CNN experiments on the pinned ImageNet-100 dataset. [32, 64, 128] means three convolutional blocks with those output-channel widths.
Shared settings: 128 x 128 images, batch size 64, AdamW, weight decay 0.0001, dropout 0, supplied augmentation, and no pretrained weights. Seed 0 was used initially; seed 1 is identified below.

MAIN FINDINGS
- Four workers greatly improved throughput.
- Higher learning rates improved five-epoch learning in the tested configurations.
- Additional depth and width improved validation accuracy.
- Width gains repeated across seeds: +5.30 percentage points for seed 0 and +5.20 for seed 1. Two-seed mean accuracy: 56.59% narrow versus 61.84% wide, both with decay after epoch 8.
- Decay helped the tested 15-epoch run. Later decay gave a modest, unreplicated improvement.
- Longer training helped with diminishing returns. Short plateaus did not establish a hard accuracy ceiling.
- No clear sustained overfitting was established. Training uses augmentation and averages predictions during learning, so its metrics are not directly equivalent to validation metrics.
- Equal epochs do not mean equal runtime across architectures. Report runtime alongside accuracy.
